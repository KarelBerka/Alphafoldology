### #1071. [Tool Submission]: MultimerMapper - Generation, Integration, and Visual Analysis of AlphaFold-Multimer Protein Complexes ([GitHub Issue #1071](https://github.com/KarelBerka/Alphafoldology/issues/1071))

- **Tool Name**: `MultimerMapper`
- **Platform**: `GitHub`
- **Category**: `Structure Prediction` (Parent: `AlphaFold-Multimer`)
- **Target URL / DOI**: [https://github.com/elviorodriguez/MultimerMapper](https://github.com/elviorodriguez/MultimerMapper)
- **Tags**: `MultimerMapper, AlphaFold-Multimer, protein-complex, stoichiometry, ppi-analysis, conformational-analysis, visualization`

**Description & Scientific Impact**:
MultimerMapper is an automated bioinformatics tool and visualization suite designed for the high-throughput generation, topological integration, analysis, and visual inspection of large protein assemblies modeled with AlphaFold-Multimer. The framework resolves combinatorial subunit stoichiometries, automates multi-chain pairing pipelines, and computes interface quality metrics (ipTM, PAE inter-chain matrices, and buried surface areas) across diverse oligomeric states.

By unifying multi-chain MSA extraction with programmatic interface assessment, MultimerMapper facilitates the systematic exploration of protein-protein interaction networks and quaternary structure predictions. It provides publication-ready visualizations, interface residue contact mapping, and clustering of predicted complex conformations.

**Key Innovations**:
- **Automated Stoichiometry Combinatorics**: Streamlines multi-chain MSA preparation and submission for varying stoichiometric ratios within multi-subunit complexes.
- **PAE & ipTM Topological Analytics**: Calculates cross-chain PAE scores, buried surface areas, and interface hydrogen-bonding networks to filter plausible quaternary assemblies.
- **Interactive Complex Mapping**: Generates intuitive 2D interface heatmaps and 3D visual outputs for rapid identification of high-confidence interaction interfaces.

- **Strengths**: Simplifies the complex multi-step workflow of generating and evaluating AlphaFold-Multimer models for multi-subunit assemblies, offering robust contact statistics.
- **Limitations**: Requires AlphaFold-Multimer output files and local Python/ChimeraX dependencies for 3D visual rendering.

### #1072. [Tool Submission]: miniprotein-binder-rfdiffusion - End-to-End Generative Pipeline for Target-Conditioned Miniprotein Binders ([GitHub Issue #1072](https://github.com/KarelBerka/Alphafoldology/issues/1072))

- **Tool Name**: `miniprotein-binder-rfdiffusion`
- **Platform**: `GitHub`
- **Category**: `Protein Design` (Parent: `RFdiffusion`)
- **Target URL / DOI**: [https://github.com/izgys/miniprotein-binder-rfdiffusion](https://github.com/izgys/miniprotein-binder-rfdiffusion)
- **Tags**: `miniprotein-binder, RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN, AlphaFold, de-novo-design, binder-design, structural-bioinformatics`

**Description & Scientific Impact**:
miniprotein-binder-rfdiffusion is an open-source, automated end-to-end computational pipeline developed by Izgys for the generative design and rigorous multi-stage validation of de novo miniprotein binders targeting viral glycoproteins and cell-surface receptors. The workflow unifies target interface cropping, RFdiffusion backbone generative trajectories, ProteinMPNN combinatorial sequence design, and AlphaFold self-consistency co-folding into a cohesive automated system.

The framework automatically ranks generated candidate designs through interface energy metrics, shape complementarity (Sc), buried solvent-accessible surface area (bSASA), and AlphaFold complex confidence (pLDDT > 85, ipTM > 0.85, interface RMSD < 1.5 Å), accelerating de novo binder screening campaigns.

**Key Innovations**:
- **Automated Multi-Stage Binder Generation**: Seamlessly connects RFdiffusion backbone generation, ProteinMPNN sequence optimization, and AlphaFold complex validation.
- **Multi-Parametric Filter Ranking**: Filters tens of thousands of generated decoys using composite biophysical metrics including shape complementarity and buried SASA.
- **Reproducible Target Scaffolding**: Provides pre-configured target bounding configurations and hotspot mappings for immediate deployment against therapeutic targets.

- **Strengths**: Clean, highly automated script architecture that lowers the technical hurdle for executing high-throughput RFdiffusion binder campaigns.
- **Limitations**: Requires local GPU infrastructure equipped with PyTorch and AlphaFold weight checkpoints for full pipeline execution.

### #1073. [Tool Submission]: REAPS - All-Atom Receptor-Aware Geometric Deep Learning Framework for De Novo Protein Design ([GitHub Issue #1073](https://github.com/KarelBerka/Alphafoldology/issues/1073))

- **Tool Name**: `REAPS`
- **Platform**: `GitHub`
- **Category**: `Protein Design` (Parent: `ProteinMPNN`)
- **Target URL / DOI**: [https://github.com/Mistletoe-git/REAPS](https://github.com/Mistletoe-git/REAPS)
- **Tags**: `REAPS, protein-design, receptor-aware, all-atom, geometric-deep-learning, de-novo-design, equivariant-gnn`

**Description & Scientific Impact**:
REAPS (Receptor-Aware Protein Sequence Design) is an all-atom geometric deep learning framework that models full receptor contexts during de novo protein design and sequence generation. While conventional inverse folding models frequently isolate target backbones or neglect explicit atomistic receptor environments, REAPS processes target-receptor coordinates within an SE(3)-equivariant graph neural network to preserve crucial interfacial hydrogen bonds, salt bridges, and steric packing constraints.

By conditioning autoregressive sequence generation on dense all-atom receptor graphs, REAPS produces designed binder sequences with substantially higher target specificity, lower energetic penalties, and enhanced in silico folding success rates when validated by AlphaFold 2 and ESMFold.

**Key Innovations**:
- **All-Atom Receptor Conditioning**: Fully models receptor atomic coordinates during sequence decoding, preventing steric clashes and unsatisfied interfacial donors/acceptors.
- **Equivariant Contextual Message Passing**: Uses SE(3)-equivariant layers to exchange spatial and electrostatic information across the binding cleft.
- **High Success In Silico Binder Recovery**: Achieves superior target co-folding confidence and interface recovery compared to standard receptor-agnostic models.

- **Strengths**: Directly resolves interfacial packing and chemical complementarity during sequence generation, reducing post-hoc filtering attrition.
- **Limitations**: Requires precise receptor coordinates; sensitive to substantial conformational plasticity in the unbound receptor target.

### #1074. [Tool Submission]: PESI - Paratope-Epitope Set Interaction Network for Antibody Neutralization Prediction ([GitHub Issue #1074](https://github.com/KarelBerka/Alphafoldology/issues/1074))

- **Tool Name**: `PESI`
- **Platform**: `GitHub`
- **Category**: `Antibody / Nanobody` (Parent: `Structure Analysis`)
- **Target URL / DOI**: [https://github.com/ZhangWan-byte/PESI](https://github.com/ZhangWan-byte/PESI)
- **Tags**: `PESI, antibody, paratope, epitope, neutralization, viral-escape, set-interaction, deep-learning`

**Description & Scientific Impact**:
PESI (Paratope-Epitope Set Interaction) is a structural deep learning model that predicts antibody neutralization potency and antigenic escape by modeling cross-attention interactions between structural paratope and epitope sets. Recognizing that antigen-antibody binding is driven by localized clusters of contacts rather than global average sequence properties, PESI extracts geometric residue sets from 3D structures and learns invariant set-to-set interaction matrices.

The framework accurately forecasts neutralization titers against emerging viral variants (including SARS-CoV-2 and influenza strains) and identifies key escape mutation hotspots without requiring expensive full-scale molecular dynamics simulations.

**Key Innovations**:
- **Set-to-Set Interfacial Attention**: Formulates paratope-epitope binding as an invariant set interaction problem, capturing cooperative multi-residue contacts.
- **Variant Escape Prediction**: Rapidly assesses the neutralizing impact of point mutations and indel variations across target viral antigens.
- **Multi-Scale Structural Representations**: Combines local atomic contact geometries with global protein language model sequence representations.

- **Strengths**: Highly accurate in predicting neutralization potency across viral escape variants while providing interpretable residue-pair attention weights.
- **Limitations**: Relies on structural annotations of paratope/epitope boundaries or high-confidence predicted complex structures.

- **Platform**: `GitHub`
- **Category**: `Structure Prediction` (Parent: `AlphaFold 3`)
- **Target URL / DOI**: [https://github.com/LambdaLabsML/TurboPairFormer-Quickstart](https://github.com/LambdaLabsML/TurboPairFormer-Quickstart)
- **Tags**: `TurboPairFormer, OpenFold3, AlphaFold3, H100, hopper, cuda-kernels, hpc-acceleration, inference-speedup`

**Description & Scientific Impact**:
